# Affiliate source checklist

Use this checklist before adding a real provider to DiscountHub.

## Business/legal

- Provider allows product/feed usage in apps.
- Provider allows affiliate links in mobile apps.
- Provider allows caching product data in your backend.
- Provider allows showing images, prices and discounts.
- Provider gives rules for price freshness.
- Provider has clear attribution requirements.

## Technical

- Feed format is JSON/XML/CSV or available through API.
- Feed has stable product IDs.
- Feed has current price and currency.
- Feed has product URL and affiliate URL.
- Feed has image URL.
- Feed has category or merchant category.
- Feed has availability/stock state when possible.
- Feed has shipping country or shipping region when possible.
- Feed size is reasonable for scheduled sync.

## Quality

- Discounts are real and not fake old-price inflation.
- Seller/merchant is trustworthy.
- Broken/out-of-stock products are removed quickly.
- Feed has update frequency or timestamp.
- There is a support contact or dashboard from the provider.
